# Research playbook

Always pass the payload as_of date as cutoff_date. For historical origins, discard
results that cannot be verified as available before the cutoff. Never use hindsight.

## Focused USD/CAD queries
Use one topic per query:
- Bank of Canada policy and guidance
- Federal Reserve policy and guidance
- US-Canada yield differential
- Canadian employment, CPI, GDP
- US employment, CPI, GDP
- WTI oil and OPEC policy
- Canada-US trade policy or major geopolitical shocks
- CAD risk sentiment and broad USD conditions

Prefer BoC, Fed, Statistics Canada, US government releases, EIA/OPEC materials,
and high-quality financial reporting. Search for the present state, not a prediction.
